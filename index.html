Google Chrome Standalone Package

Download for Chrome (7.93 MB)

Step 1: Download & Extract

Click above to download pms-extension-chrome-latest.zip and extract it to a folder (e.g. D:\PMS-Extension).

2

Step 2: Enable Developer Mode

Navigate to chrome://extensions/ in your address bar, then turn on Developer mode in the top-right.

3

Step 3: Load Unpacked Extension

Click Load unpacked on the top-left, select your extracted folder, and the PMS workbench is ready!

1

Step 1: Download & Extract

Click above to download pms-extension-firefox-latest.zip and extract it to a local folder.

2

Step 2: Open Add-on Debugging

In Firefox address bar, enter about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox and press Enter.

3

Step 3: Load Temporary Add-on

Click Load Temporary Add-on..., select manifest.json in your extracted folder, and PMS launches in your sidebar!
