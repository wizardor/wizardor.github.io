Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 · Operator: PMS Software Studio · Payment MoR: Paddle.com

1. Zero-Touch Payment Security (PCI-DSS Level 1)

All credit card, debit card, and financial transactions are entered directly into encrypted checkout fields managed by our Merchant of Record, Paddle. PMS servers and databases never receive, process, or store your sensitive payment card details or CVVs. Paddle maintains PCI-DSS Level 1 certified banking-grade infrastructure.

2. Cloud Data Storage & Encryption

Upon authorized user access, your project assets, document drafts, and uploaded materials are securely processed and stored within our dedicated encrypted cloud storage environments. We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest) to safeguard all active project files. PMS maintains strict confidentiality: your research materials, document assets, and drafts are never inspected, monetized, sold, or shared with unauthorized third parties.

3. Cloud Processing & Compilation Engine

All document parsing, multi-source attachment indexing, citation mapping, and high-resolution vector PDF compilation are executed securely on our cloud compilation server infrastructure. Processed assets are delivered directly and privately to the authenticated user's workspace.

4. Transaction Data Handled via Paddle Webhooks

To provision and manage your software licenses, we receive only non-sensitive transaction metadata from Paddle:

  • Paddle Order and Transaction IDs
  • Purchased product and price identifiers (e.g., Single-Project Lifetime Pass)
  • Customer email address (utilized strictly for license provisioning and technical support)
  • Subscription lifecycle flags (active, past-due, or canceled)

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Users retain full ownership of their materials and may request deletion of their project assets and account information at any time by contacting our support team at wizardor@gmail.com.
