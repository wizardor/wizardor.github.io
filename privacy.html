Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 · Operator: PMS AI Co-pilot Studio · Payment MoR: Paddle.com

1. Zero-Touch Payment Security (PCI-DSS Compliance)

All credit card, debit card, and financial transactions are entered directly into encrypted checkout fields managed by our Merchant of Record, Paddle. PMS servers, extensions, and local databases never receive, process, or store your sensitive payment card details or CVVs. Paddle maintains PCI-DSS Level 1 certified infrastructure.

2. Authorized Cloud & Bring-Your-Own-Storage (BYOS) Architecture

PMS operates locally within your browser sandbox (Chrome Manifest V3). Your research portfolios, draft documents, and compiled dossiers remain stored exclusively in your authorized Google Drive and local client-side storage. PMS does not retain, inspect, or monetize your private research materials on central servers.

3. Transaction Data Handled via Paddle Webhooks

To deliver and manage your digital software licenses, we receive only non-sensitive transaction metadata from Paddle:

  • Paddle Order and Transaction IDs
  • Purchased product and price identifiers (e.g., Single-Project Lifetime Pass)
  • Customer email address (utilized strictly for license provisioning and customer support)
  • Subscription status flags (active, canceled, or past-due)

4. Third-Party Sub-processors

We engage the following authorized sub-processors for billing and operational delivery:

  • Paddle Payments Ltd / Paddle.com Market Ltd (UK): Merchant of Record, transaction processing, and international tax compliance.
  • Google Cloud Platform: Optional client OAuth authorization and cloud storage synchronization.

5. Regulatory Non-Decision Safeguard

PMS is strictly an assistive document formatting and layout software utility. PMS does not make, calculate, or materially influence any legal, credit, employment, or immigration decisions regarding any natural person. PMS does not provide legal, financial, or regulated professional advice.

6. Contact Data Privacy Officer

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices or wish to request data deletion, contact our privacy contact at wizardor@gmail.com.
